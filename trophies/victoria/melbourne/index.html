Local showroom

How to find our Thomastown store

We are on Brock Street, which runs north off Settlement Road, east of Dalton Road and just south of the Metropolitan Ring Road (M80). If you know Harvey Norman Thomastown or JB Hi-Fi Home, you are a minute away.

By car or van (best for bulk pickups)

Bulk trophy orders are boxed and heavy, so driving is the easiest way to collect.

From the north (Craigieburn, Epping)

  1. Take the Hume Freeway (National Highway M31) south. About 22 km, 21 minutes from Craigieburn.
  2. Continue towards Dalton Road and Settlement Road.
  3. Turn onto Brock Street. We're at 11/8–20.

From the city and inner suburbs

  1. Take the M2, then the M80 Metropolitan Ring Road. About 32 km, 35 minutes from the CBD.
  2. Leave the Ring Road at Dalton Road and head south to Settlement Road.
  3. Turn onto Brock Street. We're at 11/8–20.

From Reservoir and nearby

  1. Head north on Cheddar Road or Dalton Road. About 4.5 km, 9 minutes.
  2. Continue to Settlement Road.
  3. Turn onto Brock Street. We're at 11/8–20.

From the west (Sunbury, Tarneit, Werribee)

  1. Join the Western Ring Road (M80 Metropolitan Ring Road) heading east.
  2. Exit at Dalton Road and head south to Settlement Road.
  3. Turn onto Brock Street. We're at 11/8–20.

By public transport

Route 902 buses run through the area. From the CBD, public transport takes roughly 35 minutes plus a short walk. Check PTV for current stops and times.

Melbourne trophy questions

Where can I pick up trophies in Melbourne?

From our Thomastown showroom at 11/8–20 Brock Street, Monday to Friday 9am to 5pm. It is off Settlement Road, near Dalton Road and the M80 Ring Road.

How fast can you engrave for a presentation night?

Standard production is 5 working days. Fast Track is 4 working days ($40 plus 10% of the order, capped at $300) and Priority Rush is 3 working days ($50 plus 15%, capped at $300). Rush pickup is $15 plus 20% of the order, capped at $300.

Do you deliver to outer suburbs like Point Cook and Pakenham?

Yes. Standard shipping is a $9.90 flat rate and express is $25, after 5 working days production.

Is engraving free?

Yes, free engraving on every order.

Can I see products before I order?

Yes. Visit the Thomastown showroom during opening hours to see samples and talk to the team.
