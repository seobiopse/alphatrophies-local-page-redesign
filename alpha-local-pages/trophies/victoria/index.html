Victoria trophy questions

Can I pick up trophies in Victoria?

Yes. Collect from our Thomastown showroom at 11/8–20 Brock Street, Monday to Friday 9am to 5pm. Standard pickup is ready 5 working days after you order, with no pickup surcharge.

How much is delivery across Victoria?

Standard shipping is a $9.90 flat rate after 5 working days production. Express shipping is a $25 flat rate.

How fast can I get trophies for a presentation night?

Fast Track is 4 working days ($40 plus 10% of the order, capped at $300). Priority Rush is 3 working days ($50 plus 15% of the order, capped at $300). Rush pickup is $15 plus 20% of the order, capped at $300.

Is engraving free?

Yes, free engraving on every order.
